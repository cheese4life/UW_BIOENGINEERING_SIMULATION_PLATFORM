Device AxisDevice.MotionControllerConfiguration

An interface for reading and modifying Motion Controller Configuration.

Fields

PhaseAngleRaw Gets the raw phase angle value reported by the motion controller, used for alignment, calibration, or diagnostic purposes.
PhaseAngleDeg Gets the phase angle in degrees as reported by the motion controller, useful for alignment, calibration, or display purposes.


Fields

PhaseAngleRaw
Gets the raw phase angle value reported by the motion controller, used for alignment, calibration, or diagnostic purposes.
  • PhaseAngleRaw number readonly

Usage:

    local rawAngle = ZAxis.MotionControllerConfiguration.PhaseAngleRaw
print("Raw phase angle:", rawAngle)
PhaseAngleDeg
Gets the phase angle in degrees as reported by the motion controller, useful for alignment, calibration, or display purposes.
  • PhaseAngleDeg number readonly

Usage:

    local angleDeg = ZAxis.MotionControllerConfiguration.PhaseAngleDeg
print("Phase angle (°):", angleDeg)
